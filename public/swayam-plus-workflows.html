Student Flow
End-to-end journey from discovery to certification. All registrations are D2C only.
Phase 1 — Discovery & Registration
ENTRY POINT · D2C ONLY
1
Browse Platform
Student lands on Swayam Plus and browses courses without logging in. No registration required to explore.
2
D2C Registration
All student accounts are created via D2C only — no HEI or partner can create student accounts. Registration captures personal details, ABC number, and APAAR number. Both ABC and APAAR are mandatory. Data consent is captured at this stage.
ABC — mandatoryAPAAR — mandatoryD2C only — no proxy registrationData consent captured
3
OTP Login
All logins are OTP-based to the registered email ID. No username/password system. Applies to all subsequent logins.
OTP to registered email
Phase 2 — Course Selection & Payment
TRANSACTION FLOW · THREE FEE MODELS
4
Select Course
Student selects a course listed on Swayam Plus. Swayam Plus is the listing and aggregation platform. Actual learning happens on partner LMS (Model B) or Swayam Plus LMS (Model A). Courses fall into three fee types — fully free (no cost for content or certification), free with paid assessment (content free, separate fee for assessment and certification), or fully paid (fee for content access; assessment may be included or charged separately).
Fully free — content + cert at no costFree access + paid assessmentFully paid course
5
Payment on Swayam Plus
All payments are collected at Swayam Plus end. Invoice generated immediately upon successful payment. Partner is notified of new enrollment.
Invoice auto-generatedPartner notified
6
Refund Policy
Student must request a refund within 3 working days of payment or before completing the course — whichever occurs first. Refund requests are reviewed on a case-by-case basis. Learner may be asked to provide screenshots of any technical issues faced on Swayam Plus or the partner portal. Swayam Plus reserves the right to refuse requests deemed fraudulent or against terms and conditions. Once a genuine refund is approved, 100% of payment is returned to the student via the original payment method. Swayam Plus is not responsible for gateway processing duration.
3 working days from paymentCase-by-case reviewScreenshots may be requested100% refund if approvedFraudulent requests refused
Phase 3 — LMS Access & Course Consumption
LEARNING PHASE · 6-MONTH WINDOW FROM FIRST LOGIN
7
LMS Access — Model A (Swayam Plus LMS)
If course is hosted on Swayam Plus LMS, student accesses directly within the platform. No redirect. 6-month clock starts at first login.
Model A — no redirect6-month clock starts at first login
8
LMS Access — Model B (Partner LMS via SSO)
If course is hosted on partner LMS, student is redirected via SSO using registered email. Partner provisions access within max 24-hour TAT. If SSO fails, grievance is auto-generated on student dashboard. 6-month clock starts at first login.
Model B — SSO redirectMax 24-hour TATSSO failure → auto grievance6-month clock starts at first login
9
Course Access Rules
Student must always access the course through Swayam Plus. Student can only see Swayam Plus-listed courses — not other courses on the partner LMS. Course cannot be paused — must be completed within 6-month window from first login.
6-month window from first loginNo pause/resumeSwayam Plus-listed courses only
10
Access Expiry
If student does not complete course within 6 months of first login, access is terminated. Automated email sent 30 days before expiry as warning, and again on expiry communicating termination.
Warning email 30 days beforeTermination email on expiry
Phase 4 — Assessment & Certification
POST-COMPLETION · THIRD PARTY ASSESSMENT
11
Assessment Registration
On course completion, student gets option to sign up for assessment on Swayam Plus. Swayam Plus validates course completion — for Model A courses, platform validates internally. For Model B courses, partner confirms completion. Access to assessment is blocked until completion is validated.
Model A — platform validates internallyModel B — partner confirms
12
Assessment Payment & Scheduling
Separate payment required for assessment and certification. Invoice generated upon payment. Scheduling assistant on Swayam Plus helps student find an exam slot. Assessment is conducted by a third party.
Invoice generatedScheduling on Swayam PlusAssessment by 3rd party
13
Rescheduling & No-Show Policy
Student can reschedule up to 48 hours before scheduled slot. No reschedule allowed after that. No-show means student must pay again for a fresh booking. No refund for cancellation or no-show under any circumstance.
Reschedule: up to 48 hrs beforeNo show = pay againNo refund
14
Exam Reminder
Automated email reminder sent to student 24 hours before the scheduled examination.
Email 24 hrs before exam
15
Certificate Generation
If student passes (passing criteria defined by partner), a verifiable certificate is generated by Swayam Plus. Certificate is auto-uploaded to ABC portal, emailed to student, and available for download on student dashboard at any time. Student can share certificate link on social media.
Auto-upload to ABC portalEmail with linkDashboard download anytimeSocial share enabledCertificate issued by Swayam Plus

Assessment Failure
No reattempts allowed. Student must pay again for a fresh assessment registration and schedule a new slot.
No reattemptsMust pay and rebook
Phase 5 — Feedback
TRIGGERED AT COMPLETION OR 6-MONTH EXPIRY — WHICHEVER IS EARLIER
17
Feedback Form Trigger
A feedback form is automatically triggered at whichever event occurs first — course completion or 6-month access expiry. The form is mandatory and must be submitted before the student's dashboard marks the course as fully closed.
Triggered at completion OR 6-month expiry — whichever is earlierMandatory form
18
Feedback Form — Two Sections
Section 1 — Platform Experience: Student rates and comments on Swayam Plus as a platform — navigation, payment experience, support, and overall usability. Section 2 — Partner & Course Experience: Student rates the course content quality, instructor effectiveness, LMS experience, and overall satisfaction with the partner's course.
Section 1: Platform experienceSection 2: Partner & course experience
19
Feedback Routing
Platform feedback is reviewed by Super Admin for platform improvement. Partner and course feedback is aggregated per course and tracked against the 75% satisfaction threshold. Negative feedback on a specific course is shared with the partner by Super Admin requesting rectification.
Platform feedback → Super AdminPartner feedback → aggregated + trackedNegative feedback shared with partner

Partner Flow
From onboarding through course delivery, revenue reconciliation, and exit
Phase 1 — Partner Onboarding
ENTRY POINT · SOP SEQUENCE
Note: Steps 1–5 below can run in parallel, not necessarily in linear sequence.
1
Expression of Interest (EOI)
Interested organisations initiate the process by submitting an EOI form on the Swayam Plus website. The form captures basic organisational information — name, establishment date, existing partnerships, area of expertise, course content capabilities, and financial information. Once submitted, the Swayam Plus team evaluates and schedules an introductory call.
Entry point for all new partnersBasic org info + financial info captured
2
Due Diligence Form
Partner fills a comprehensive due diligence form capturing organisational background, operational capacity, financial stability, partnership history, and readiness to deliver online courses at scale. Super Admin evaluates and approves or rejects with reason.
Legitimacy, experience, financial stability assessed
3
Rejection Path
Partners rejected at EOI or due diligence stage receive immediate notification with reason. Can reapply only in the next cycle.
Immediate email with reasonReapply: next cycle only
4
Course Details Form
Approved partners provide preliminary information about proposed courses — content overview, delivery format, duration, and intended learner outcomes. This feeds into the course review and onboarding decision.
Content overview + delivery format + outcomes
5
Course Review & Onboarding Decision
Submitted course information is reviewed by the designated academic committee. A formal onboarding decision is communicated to the applicant. Approved courses proceed to technical integration.
Formal onboarding decision communicated
6
Technical Integration Discussion
A technical discussion is scheduled to outline the course onboarding process and API integration framework. API integration scope is determined at this stage based on cycle requirements.
API integration scoped here
7
MOU Execution via Legality + Manual Upload to Swayam Plus
A draft MOU is shared with the partner for review and legal vetting. IIT Madras legal team may be consulted for specific concerns. MOU is signed digitally via "Legality" — a third-party digital signing portal external to Swayam Plus. Automated emails sent to the designated signee and witness for digital signature. Once signed on Legality, the executed MOU is manually uploaded to Swayam Plus by the Super Admin. Only after the upload is confirmed on Swayam Plus does the partner get access to upload course metadata. MOU is valid for 3 years with a reminder sent 3 months before expiry.
Signed via Legality (external 3rd party)Manually uploaded to Swayam Plus post-signing3-year validity3-month expiry reminderUpload unlocks metadata access
8
MOU Renewal (after 3 years)
MOU renewal is a renewal only — no fresh due diligence required unless Super Admin specifically flags it. Existing courses and enrolled students are not disrupted during the renewal period. Renewal MOU is also signed via Legality and manually uploaded to Swayam Plus.
No fresh due diligenceNo disruption to courses or studentsSuper Admin can flag for re-diligenceRenewal also via Legality + manual upload
Phase 2 — EOI, Metadata & Hosting Model
EVERY 6 MONTHS
6
EOI Cycle Opens
EOI cycle opens once every 6 months. All partners receive email intimation with form link. Partners fill a separate EOI form for each course. Rejected EOI submissions can only be resubmitted in the next cycle.
Every 6 monthsOne form per courseRejection: next cycle only
7
Course Metadata Upload & Hosting Model Selection
Once EOI courses are approved, partner uploads final course metadata and selects their hosting model. This decision is locked for the duration of the active enrollment cycle. Every metadata change requires Super Admin approval. Super Admin decides on a case-by-case basis whether a metadata change triggers PIC re-review.
Every change needs Super Admin approvalPIC re-review at Super Admin discretionHosting model locked while enrollments are active
7a
Model A — Hosted on Swayam Plus LMS
Course is hosted directly on Swayam Plus LMS. Each partner gets a separate, isolated LMS instance — no cross-partner data visibility under any circumstance. Partner has full access to their instance: edit content, download student data, access all analytics. Revenue split: 50% partner / 50% Swayam Plus.
50% partner / 50% Swayam PlusFull LMS access — isolated instanceNo cross-partner data visibility
7b
Model B — Redirect to Partner LMS
Course hosted on partner's own LMS. Student redirected via SSO from Swayam Plus. Partner manages their own LMS entirely. Revenue split: 80% partner / 20% Swayam Plus.
80% partner / 20% Swayam PlusSSO redirect
8
Hosting Model Change Policy
A partner may request to switch hosting model (A↔B) only between EOI cycles — not while a course is live with active enrollments. Request must be flagged to Super Admin. Change takes effect from the next EOI cycle. All existing enrolled students continue on the original model until their 6-month window closes.
No mid-cycle model switchChange effective from next EOI cycleExisting students: original model until expiry
9
Course Approval / Rejection
After PIC review, Super Admin approves or rejects courses. Partner notified with reason for every action. Approved courses valid for 2 years. Reminder sent 3 months before renewal. When a course is marked for PIC review (including re-reviews), it is blocked for new enrollments until review is complete. Existing students retain access.
2-year validity3-month renewal reminderBlocked for new enrollments during PIC reviewExisting students unaffected

Phase 4 — Performance & Exit
PERFORMANCE MONITORING
14
Performance Threshold — Student Volume
Partner courses must clock 1,000 students in 6 months. If threshold is not met, partner receives 30-day notice with email intimation. Course is removed after 30 days. No appeal allowed.
1,000 students / 6 months30-day removal noticeNo appeal
14a
Performance Threshold — Satisfaction Rate
Partner must maintain a minimum 75% positive satisfaction rate based on aggregated student feedback across all feedback submissions for the course. Satisfaction rate is calculated continuously as feedback forms are submitted — triggered at each student's course completion or 6-month expiry, whichever is earlier. If the satisfaction rate drops below 75%, Super Admin is alerted.
Minimum 75% positive feedback requiredAggregated across all submissions per courseBelow 75% triggers alert to Super Admin
14b
Satisfaction Breach — 30-Day Warning Period
On breach of the 75% threshold, Super Admin issues a 30-day warning to the partner with the aggregated negative feedback and reason. Students enrolled in the course are also notified that the course is under review and may be removed in 30 days. No new enrollments are allowed during the 30-day warning window. Partner must address feedback issues and demonstrate improvement within 30 days. Super Admin reviews and decides whether to lift the warning or proceed with removal.
30-day warning to partnerStudents notified of potential removalNo new enrollments during warning periodSuper Admin reviews at end of 30 days
14c
Satisfaction Breach — Outcome
If partner demonstrates improvement within 30 days and satisfaction rate recovers, Super Admin lifts the warning and course is restored for new enrollments. If not resolved within 30 days, course is removed from the platform.
Resolved → warning lifted, enrollments restoredUnresolved → course removed
15
Existing Students on Removed Course
Students who have already paid retain access for their 6-month window from first login regardless of course removal reason — volume threshold or satisfaction breach. No new registrations allowed for flagged or removed courses.
Existing access continuesNo new registrations
16
Voluntary Exit
Partner can choose to exit voluntarily. From exit date, no new registrations allowed. Students with paid enrollments as on the record date of exit get either continued 6-month access or full refund. Partner commits to one option and must honour it.
No new registrations from exit dateContinued access OR full refund for existing students

Tagging Rules
HOW EMAIL / SYSTEM / BOTH IS DECIDED
EMAIL only — time-sensitive or must reach the user regardless of whether they are logged in. Missing it has a real consequence. Sent to registered email ID via email service.
SYSTEM only — purely operational, low urgency, relevant only within an active workflow the user is managing on the platform. Visible as an in-app notification or dashboard update when user logs in. No email sent.
BOTH — high-stakes event where missing it has a financial, legal, access, or deadline consequence. Must reach the user on email AND be tracked inside the platform as a persistent in-app notification for reference and audit.

ALL LEARNERS
MASTER LIST
1
Trigger
Email auto-added immediately upon successful registration on Swayam Plus — regardless of role selected.
2
Scope
Every registered user on the platform. The master list. All other DLs are subsets of this list.
3
Opt-out
User can opt out of ALL LEARNERS entirely — this means no marketing communication from any DL. Or they can opt out of specific sub-lists only while remaining on ALL LEARNERS.
Opt-out of master = no marketing at all

Email stays on the list after course access expires by default. Removed only on user's explicit opt-out choice.
5
6-Monthly Profile Verification
Every 6 months, an automated email goes to ALL LEARNERS asking them to verify and update their profile details. Any role changes made in response update the relevant sub-DLs in real time upon profile save.
